# 300 Calorie Tuna Toast and Apple

A savory, high-protein tuna toast with fresh fruit on the side for sweetness, crunch, and fiber.

## Ingredients

- 1 slice whole wheat bread, about 34 g and 90 calories
- 1 5 oz can tuna in water, drained
- Mustard, to taste
- 1 medium apple, about 180 g edible portion
- Optional: black pepper or everything bagel seasoning

## Instructions

1. Toast the bread.
2. Drain the tuna well.
3. Mix the tuna with mustard.
4. Put the tuna on the toast, or eat it alongside the toast.
5. Serve with the apple on the side.

## Notes

- A typical can of tuna, the bread, and a medium apple total about 300 calories.
- Do not add mayo by default; it pushes the snack above 300 calories.
- The apple is already covered by the fresh-fruit staple and adds sweetness and fiber. Baby carrots can replace it when more volume and a fully savory snack sound better.
