# 800 Calorie Pantry Chicken Tomato Pasta Dinner

A single-serving pantry dinner built around whole wheat pasta, canned chicken, Rao's pasta sauce, beans, black olives, and spinach or frozen broccoli.

## Ingredients

- 85 g dry whole wheat pasta
- 1 5 oz can chicken, drained
- 185 g Rao's pasta sauce
- 130 g black beans, drained and rinsed
- 30 g canned black olives, drained and sliced (pitted)
- 100 g spinach or other dark leafy greens, or 100 g frozen broccoli florets
- Iodized salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Boil water. Add salt. Add pasta and cook until al dente, following package directions.
2. If using frozen broccoli, microwave 100 g from frozen for about 3 minutes, until hot.
3. Stir the drained pasta, drained chicken, and black beans into the sauce. Add the spinach, other leafy greens, or cooked broccoli.
4. (Optional) Microwave the entire bowl until the leafy greens are wilted, if using, and everything is hot.
5. Top with the drained, sliced black olives before serving.

## Notes

- This is the pantry version of a pasta dinner. It uses canned chicken rather than a cooked chicken breast.
- Relative to the first 800-calorie version, 15 g less dry pasta (roughly 50 calories) makes room for 30 g black olives (roughly 50 calories). The Rao's sauce stays at 185 g to keep the pasta saucy.
- Compared with the original 700-calorie version, the sauce is increased from 125 g to 185 g and black olives are added. Exact calories depend on the product labels.
- Frozen broccoli is a direct alternative to the spinach. Microwave it separately before adding it to the sauce.
- Do not add oil by default; the pasta, beans, chicken, and sauce already bring this close to the 800-calorie target.
