# 300 Calorie Miso Two-Egg Soup Snack

A warm, savory snack for when tofu is unavailable, with two boiled eggs and toast providing most of the protein and calories.

## Ingredients

- 18 g dashi-flavored miso powder or paste
- 3 g dried wakame
- Scallions, to taste
- 400 g water
- 2 boiled eggs
- 1 slice whole wheat bread, about 34 g and 90 calories
- 50 g baby carrots on the side

## Instructions

1. Heat the water until hot but not aggressively boiling.
2. Stir in the miso until dissolved.
3. Add the wakame and heat gently until it has expanded.
4. Add scallions.
5. Toast the bread.
6. Eat the soup with the two boiled eggs, toast, and baby carrots.

## Notes

- The soup itself is intentionally light; the eggs, toast, and carrots bring the snack close to 300 calories.
- This version uses two eggs, which can make weekly egg planning line up more cleanly with common package sizes.
- 18 g miso is the standard amount. 13 g may taste weak.
- If the bread differs substantially from 90 calories per slice, adjust the carrots or accept a small calorie difference.
- This is especially useful when you want something warm and salty rather than sweet.
